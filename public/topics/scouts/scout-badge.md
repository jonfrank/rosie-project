## Questions

1. Can you work out what this object is?

This is a Scout badge - maybe you have some of these yourself! During the war, only boys were in the Scouts, and girls were in the Guides. Until girls were allowed to join in 1992, the Scouts were known as the Boy Scouts.

2. What do you think the image on the badge represents? Think about the shape and colour.

The image represents fire. The image had to be simple to make the badge easy and cheap to manufacture. Modern badges are much more detailed.

3. Why do you think a Scout would have been awarded this badge during World War Two?

This badge was a Firefighter badge. Scouts had to know what to do if there was a fire, how to call the fire brigade, and how to put out different types of fire. Scouts can earn a similar badge today, called the Fire Safety badge.
