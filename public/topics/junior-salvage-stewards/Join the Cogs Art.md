## Questions

1. What can you see in this poster?

'Cogs' was the nickname given to the Junior Salvage Stewards. These were children who signed up to collect 'salvage' from local households, which would be taken to be used in factories.

Salvaging means saving something to be used again. During World War Two, certain types of household waste, or salvage, were collected to be reused, just like our recycling is today!

2. Look at the children's expressions. How do you think they feel about being part of the Cogs?

Did you notice the children smiling? Maybe you said they look happy, excited or even proud to be Cogs.

3. How does this poster encourage children to join the Cogs?

This is a propaganda poster created by the British government. Propaganda tries to influence people's beliefs and actions.

This poster presents joining the Cogs as a fun role where children can be part of the war effort, and earn a badge as a reward.
