## Questions

1. How is this different to the adult's gas mask?

You probably noticed the brighter colours on the child's mask. The eye sections are a little bit different, and the mask is much smaller than the adult's gas mask. Did you notice any other differences?

2. Why do you think this child's gas mask is different to the adult one?

Adult gas masks were heavy, so young children needed smaller, lighter gas masks. 

Gas masks could be scary. Giving colourful gas masks to younger children, like this red 'Mickey Mouse' one, helped to make wearing it a bit more fun. 

3. What do you think this gas mask is made from?

Gas masks were mostly made from rubber and metal. These materials were used to make lots of important objects during World War Two, so they were in high demand.
