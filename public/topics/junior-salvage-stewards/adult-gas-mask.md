## Questions

1. Do you know what this object is?

This is a gas mask. Gas masks were carried by everybody in Britain during World War Two, to be protect people in case Nazi Germany dropped a gas bomb on Britain. This didn't happen, but people were prepared.

2. How do you think gas masks were worn?

Gas masks were worn on your head, covering your face. The window lets you see, and the filter at the bottom helps you to breathe without letting in the gas. The straps are adjustable, and gas masks came in different sizes - did you spot which size this one is?

3. Gas masks were carried in boxes with string straps. How would you feel if you had to carry this around everywhere you went?
